The work we do

SCE Projects provide a high quality electrical and refrigeration service to the Central Coast and Sydney areas. Licensed electricians and refrigeration mechanics, covering Level 2 contestable works and the services below.

01

Level 2 ASP

SCE Projects are accredited service providers and are qualified to work in the Ausgrid area, providing power from the network to the customer. Our trained staff will carry out the paperwork and the necessary application and qualifications to get your electrical needs sorted promptly with minimal issues. We are authorised to perform Level 2 contestable works in categories 1 through 4.

  • All paperwork for approvals
  • All metering including flat rate, time of use, off peak and solar
  • Overhead single and three phase connections and replacements
  • Underground services
  • Underground to overhead connections (UGOH)
  • Upgrade of supply 200A, 400A
  • Temporary builder supply, including pole and switchboard hire
  • Installation of poles and pillars
  • Replacement of damaged and defective poles
  • Permanent disconnection of supply
  • Switchboard upgrades
  • Defect repairs
  • Service fuse installation
  • Barge fuse installation
  • Disconnection and reconnection of supply
  • Point of attachment installation and removal
  • Bare conductor screening such as tiger tails and low voltage mats hire
